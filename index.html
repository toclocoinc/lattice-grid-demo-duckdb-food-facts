4.8 million products, explored like a notebook, in a tab

Open Food Facts is a shared database of food products from around the world. This page holds 4.8 million of them in one file and asks it questions live in your browser, the way you would in a notebook on a laptop: filter by country, brand and Nutri-Score, search by name, group by category for medians, and watch the chart follow. Every step is a query, and the SQL it ran is shown below.

Starting. The first visit downloads the query engine, so it takes a moment.

Data: Open Food Facts product database, by Open Food Facts contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL), with the individual contents under the Database Contents Licence (DbCL). Product images are not used. This page shows a column extract of the database; data derived from it and shared is shared under the ODbL, with the same attribution. Nutrient values that cannot be real (more than 100 g of sugar or salt in 100 g, more than 900 kcal) are left blank.
